04 / Field notes

What the research changed.

The operating plan improved by testing common wholesaling advice against Tennessee law and Memphis-specific deal risk.

Note 01

Tennessee regulates the assignment process.

Public Chapter 72 requires key disclosures inside the purchase agreement itself, in bold, large-format text. The seller also needs written notice at least three business days before an assignment takes effect.

Note 02

Earnest money norms are local.

Early national guidance suggested $100. Tennessee-specific sources point to $500 to $2,000, so the actual Memphis norm will be confirmed with local title companies before the first offer.

Note 03

Title risk starts with the seller story.

Heirship properties are a leading Memphis deal-killer. Ownership and all heirs must be verified before time or money goes into marketing the contract.

Note 04

The first fee is operating income.

Assignment fees are ordinary income, not capital gains. Expense tracking, quarterly estimates, and CPA guidance belong in the system from deal one.
